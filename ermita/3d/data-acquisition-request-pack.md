# Ermita imagery and elevation data request pack

This file contains ready-to-send requests. They are templates only; no request has been submitted and no approval or purchase is implied.

## 1. LiPAD / Phil-LiDAR data request

**Portal:** https://lipad.dream.upd.edu.ph/?id=14  
**Contact:** lipad@dream.upd.edu.ph  
**Area:** Ermita / Rizal Park, City of Manila, Philippines. Use the project's current AOI polygon as a shapefile attachment; the approximate search bounds currently recorded in the project are 120.965–121.005 E, 14.560–14.605 N.

**Subject:** Data access request — LiDAR/orthophoto coverage for Ermita, Manila

Dear LiPAD / Phil-LiDAR team,

I am developing a research and visualization project that creates a georeferenced 3D representation of Ermita and Rizal Park in Manila. I would like to confirm whether existing datasets cover the attached area of interest.

If available, I request guidance on obtaining:
1. LiDAR-derived DSM and DTM (GeoTIFF or equivalent), including pixel size, vertical datum, acquisition date, and accuracy metadata;
2. LiDAR orthophotos/orthomosaics, including ground sampling distance and acquisition date;
3. classified LiDAR point clouds (LAZ/LAS), including point density and class definitions;
4. any building/roof vectors or height products derived from the survey.

Please advise the requester-registration and data-request steps, any approval requirements or fees, and the terms governing local storage, derivative 3D modeling, public display, and redistribution of derived outputs. I will preserve source attribution and follow the applicable license and restrictions.

Thank you.

**Subject:** Request for Ermita, Manila DSM/DTM and orthophoto coverage, metadata, and quotation

Thank you.

## Required tracking after any response or download

For each provider reply, save the email/letter or a redacted non-sensitive record of the decision, update `source-manifest.csv`, and record:
- provider and exact dataset/layer name;
- AOI/tile IDs and acquisition date;
- original filename, format, size, checksum, CRS, resolution and vertical datum;
- license/EULA and attribution;
- download date, transformations and validation results;
- whether raw files may be committed to GitHub, or only a permitted derivative/metadata can be stored.

**Do not claim data acquisition is complete until the actual file is downloaded and validated.**
