This is a connected, continuous street scene—not a slideshow. The road and sidewalks run through the whole route; real photographs from the Mabini / Malate area are used as facade textures. It is an early reconstruction, not a surveyed 1:1 replica.

Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to walk, mouse to look. Mobile: drag to look, use the on-screen arrows to move.
