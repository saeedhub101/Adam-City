# Street signs

street_signs.csv is for sign observations, including street-name blades, traffic/regulatory signs, directional signs, building-number plates and other street-level identifiers.

For each observation, capture the displayed text exactly, language/script, sign type, approximate coordinates, nearest intersection, observation date and source/photo rights. Keep uncertain readings marked as uncertain in notes.

Do not copy/rehost Google Street View or other proprietary imagery unless the relevant terms explicitly permit it. Original field photos should include photographer/rights metadata.

## Current status
The CSV contains a schema only; no sign observations or photos have been imported yet.
