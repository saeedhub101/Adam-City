# Stores and businesses

The primary table is stores.csv. Keep one record per business location, not one record per brand. Include restaurants, cafés, retail stores, convenience stores, pharmacies, hotels and other public-facing businesses. Hospitals and clinics may also appear in the broad place dataset; maintain a dedicated health-facilities export when enough records are collected.

## Collection rules
- Prefer licensed OpenStreetMap feature exports for initial broad coverage.
- Verify names and addresses with official business pages or other reliable sources where possible.
- Record access/source date and reuse terms.
- Do not infer opening hours, phone numbers or coordinates.
- A chain with multiple branches should have a separate record for each branch.
- Use candidate until a record has been checked; avoid claiming the table is comprehensive.

## Current status
The CSV currently contains the field schema only. No store records have been imported into this table yet.
