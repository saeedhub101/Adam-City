## Tree inventory fields visible in NPDC's 2024 workbook

The official workbook preview shows fields including tree number, common name, scientific name, family name, tree classification, fruit-bearing status, DBH, height, longitude, latitude, tree defects, health, structural integrity, overall risk rating and remarks. These are candidate fields to preserve when the original workbook is downloaded and parsed.

**Coordinate caution:** the workbook preview presents longitude and latitude as degree/minute/second text (for example, `E 120 58 48.34` and `N 14 34 58.51`). Parse these carefully into decimal degrees only after validating the source columns; do not copy the preview's shifted/ambiguous cells blindly. Keep original coordinate text in the raw dataset.

The online workbook preview contains individual records such as AG86 (Neem tree), AG131 (Alagau), AG88 (Mango), AG87 (Banaba), and others. This note is not a complete transcription of the workbook. The original workbook must be acquired, all populated rows extracted, and its record count compared with NPDC's stated counts before claiming full coverage.
