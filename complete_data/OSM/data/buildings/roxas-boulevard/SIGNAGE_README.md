# Roxas Boulevard signage overlay

The companion asset `roxas-boulevard-signage.glb` is generated by
`ermita/scripts/generate_roxas_boulevard_signage.py` during the Pages build.

- Building names, business names/brands and address numbers come from the repository's OSM building and address datasets.
- The overlay is aligned to the origin in `roxas-bulivard-buildings-origin.json`.
- `roxas-boulevard-signage-report.json` records generated panels and source IDs.
- Panels are approximate placements. Brand-colour styling is not an exact logo, and the overlay is not a photo-verified reconstruction of every facade.
- Buildings with no mapped name, business point or address number are listed as unlabelled rather than assigned invented names.
- KartaView imagery is not used by this generator.
