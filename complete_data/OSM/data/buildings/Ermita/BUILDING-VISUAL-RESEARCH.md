# Building visual research queue

This queue includes each exported building-part record that has no accepted OSM link or has an ambiguous spatial candidate. It provides coordinate-specific Google Maps and Mapillary entry points plus a Wikimedia Commons image search. These links are research shortcuts, not proof that a photo depicts the exact building.

## Review rules

- Inspect coordinate-specific imagery before recording an identity or façade attributes.
- Confirm a Commons image depicts the same building and check its license/attribution.
- Candidate OSM IDs and names are unconfirmed hints, not accepted links.
- Fill review_decision and review_notes only after visual/source verification. Do not infer exact colors, materials, windows, signs, or addresses from coordinates alone.
- This workflow does not alter GLBs, geometry, positions, IDs, heights, or existing colors; it does not claim imagery has already been verified.
